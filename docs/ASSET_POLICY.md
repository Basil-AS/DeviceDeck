# Icon and image policy

- `assets/icons/`: DeviceDeck's original app icon, monochrome/tray variants, and source vector files.
- `assets/device-images/`: original vector silhouettes/illustrations or assets with redistribution permission.
- Keep a `SOURCES.md` beside any imported artwork with author, source URL, license, and modifications.
- Do not extract or redistribute vendor app icons, product renders, or fonts from the supplied APKs unless the rights explicitly permit it.
- Use neutral, original device silhouettes until licensed product imagery is available.
